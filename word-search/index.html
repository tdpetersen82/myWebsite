Play Word Search online — or make your own

Word Search is the relaxing letter-grid puzzle where hidden words wait to be found among a jumble of letters. Play a ready-made themed puzzle — animals, space, ocean, food and more — or switch to Make your own and turn any word list into a puzzle you can play, share with a link, or print with an answer key. Free in your browser, no downloads, no signup.

Word Search rules

  • Find the listed words. Every word in the checklist is hidden somewhere in the grid in a straight line.
  • Select a word. Press on its first letter and drag to its last letter, then release. The highlight snaps to a straight line, so a rough swipe still reads cleanly.
  • Directions depend on the setting. Easier puzzles hide words across and down only; harder ones add diagonal and backwards words.
  • Win. Cross off every word on the list to solve the puzzle. There's no way to lose — take your time.

Word Search tips and strategy

  • Hunt first letters. Scan the grid for the starting letter of each word, then check the lines radiating out from it.
  • Look for rare letters. Words with Q, X, Z or J are the fastest to spot — there are few decoys to confuse them.
  • Sweep row by row. A steady left-to-right, top-to-bottom scan beats darting around at random.
  • Check diagonals last. They're the hardest for the eye to catch — on harder grids, deliberately look up-left and down-right too.
  • Use a hint when stuck. The hint flashes a remaining word's first letter to get you unstuck without giving it away.

Make your own word search puzzle

The built-in word search maker turns any list of words into a finished puzzle in one click — spelling lists, party themes, classroom vocabulary, baby-shower games, you name it:

  • Type your words. 2–24 words, one per line or separated by commas. Spaces and punctuation are stripped automatically, so “ice cream” becomes ICECREAM.
  • Pick a difficulty. Easy hides words across and down only — ideal for young kids. Medium adds diagonals. Hard adds backwards words for grown-ups.
  • Pick a grid size — or leave it on Auto and the maker chooses the smallest grid that fits your words comfortably.
  • Play it instantly. Your puzzle uses the same drag-to-find board as the themed puzzles, complete with the timer and hints.

Print a word search with an answer key

Every puzzle — themed or homemade — prints as a clean black-and-white worksheet: the title, the letter grid, and the word list on page one, and an answer key with every hidden word shaded on page two. Press Print under the board (or use your browser's print command) and the page chrome, buttons, and background stay off the paper. Perfect for classrooms, road trips, and rainy afternoons.

Share your puzzle with a link

Made a puzzle worth passing around? Press Copy link. The link stores your words, title, and settings, so anyone who opens it gets exactly the same grid to solve — nothing to install and no account needed on either end.

Easy, medium, and hard grids

Small (8×8) hides a handful of short words across and down only — great for kids and quick rounds. Medium (11×11) is the balanced default and adds diagonals. Large (14×14) packs in more words and adds backwards runs for a real challenge. Your fastest time for each theme-and-size combination is saved automatically.

Frequently asked

How do I play Word Search? Find every word on the list inside the letter grid. Press a word's first letter and drag to its last letter — across, down, or diagonally — then release to cross it off. Clear the list to win.

Can I make my own word search? Yes — switch to Make your own, type 2–24 words, pick a difficulty and grid size, and the maker builds a playable puzzle instantly. No account needed.

Can I print the word search? Yes — every puzzle prints as a clean black-and-white sheet with the grid and word list, plus an answer key on a second page. Use the Print button.

How do I share a puzzle I made? Press Copy link. The link stores your words and settings, so anyone who opens it gets exactly the same puzzle to solve.

Is Word Search free? Yes — playing and making puzzles are both free. No signup, no downloads, no installs.

Can words go backwards or diagonally? Yes, depending on the setting. In themed puzzles, Small grids hide words across and down only, Medium adds diagonals, and Large adds backwards words. In the maker, the Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties work the same way.

Are my best times saved? Yes — your fastest solve for each theme and grid size is stored locally in your browser.
