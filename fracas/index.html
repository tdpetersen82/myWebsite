How to play Fracas

Fracas is a maze bomber in the Bomberman tradition, set in a limestone mine. The galleries are choked with rock, and the only way through is dynamite. Lay a bundle, step clear, and two seconds later it goes off in a cross of fire that clears the rock and anything else it touches — bats, knockers, other miners, and you if you linger. Blast through the rock to find gear and the lift, clear every critter, then step into the lift to ride down to the next level.

Controls

  • ← ↑ → ↓ or W A S D — move
  • Space — lay dynamite (also Z, X, Enter)
  • P — pause
  • Two players: the blue miner uses WASD + Space, the red miner uses the arrow keys + Enter.
  • On a phone, use the on-screen pad and dynamite button.

Modes

  • Adventure. Solo levels that get denser and meaner. Each level hides the lift under one of the rocks; it opens once every critter is gone. Three lives, a three-minute clock per level, and a score that saves as your best. Run the clock out and the gas catches: sparks come hunting.
  • Battle the computer. Two miners, one gallery, last one standing takes the round. First to three rounds wins the match. With 45 seconds left the roof starts caving in from the edges, so a stand-off never lasts.
  • Two players. The same gallery, two people on one keyboard.

Gear

  • 🧨 Dynamite — lay one more bundle at a time (up to six).
  • 💥 Blast — the explosion reaches one tile further in every direction.
  • 👢 Boots — move faster. Stacks three times.
  • ⛑ Helmet — survive one hit.

In Adventure your gear carries over between levels. In Battle everyone starts each round fresh, and the gallery hides a full set to race for.

Tips & strategy

  • Never blast in a dead end. Before you light it, know which way you're leaving. A one-tile pocket with dynamite at your back is where most runs end.
  • Dynamite is a wall. Once you step off a bundle you can't walk back over it, and neither can a critter. Use one to plug a corridor between you and something nasty.
  • Corners are safety. Fire only travels in straight lines, so a step around a pillar puts you out of reach of any blast.
  • Chain them. A blast that reaches another bundle sets it off at once. Two bundles with a long fuse can be turned into one wide trap.
  • Rock shields you. The blast stops at the first rock it hits, so an unbroken boulder between you and the dynamite is as good as a wall.
  • Hunt the hunters. Knockers, the green mine goblins, chase when they see you down a corridor. Let one line up on you, lay a bundle, and step around the corner.
  • Find the lift early, take it last. It only runs once the level is clear. Know where it is, then finish the critters before you head for it.
  • In Battle, take the middle. The cave-in eats the edges first. Whoever holds the centre with more blast range usually wins the endgame.

About Fracas

The maze-bomber formula — a grid of pillars, breakable blocks, bombs on a fuse and enemies that wander in — dates to Hudson Soft's Bomberman in 1983 and its four-player battle mode on the SNES a decade later. This version takes its name from a 1996 DOS shareware game that gave the idea a fantasy makeover. Fracas is our own take, moved underground to a limestone mine: the same one-more-go loop, drawn fresh with dynamite, boulders, bats and knockers, and a computer opponent that knows better than to stand in its own blast.

Frequently asked

How do I play Fracas? Move with the arrow keys or WASD and press Space to lay dynamite. It blows two seconds later in a cross shape, clearing rock and anything standing in the blast — including you. Clear the critters, uncover the lift, and step into it to finish the level.

Can I play Fracas with two players? Yes. Battle mode is two miners on one keyboard: WASD and Space against the arrow keys and Enter. You can also battle the computer. First to three rounds wins, and the roof caves in from the edges if a round runs long.

Is Fracas free? Yes — no signup, no downloads, no installs.

Does my high score save? Yes — your best Adventure score is stored locally in your browser.
