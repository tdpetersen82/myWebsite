For classrooms and families

Nothing to install

Every game runs in the browser tab, on a Chromebook, a school laptop or the family tablet. Bookmark this page and it's ready for indoor recess, a brain break or a rainy afternoon.

No accounts, nothing collected

There is no signup and no chat. Scores and settings are saved only in that browser, so a shared machine keeps each class's records without anyone logging in.

Odd number of players?

Every game here except Hook & Ladder also plays against the computer, with easy levels for beginners. One person can keep playing while the other pair takes a turn.

Frequently asked

Do both players need their own device? No. Every game on this page is played on one screen. The arcade games share one keyboard — one player on WASD, the other on the arrow keys — and the board and dice games are pass-and-play: take your turn, then hand over the mouse or the tablet.

Which games can two people play at the same time? Hook & Ladder, Dynamine and Pong. In each one, player 1 uses W, A, S and D and player 2 uses the arrow keys, on the same keyboard. Hook & Ladder needs two players; Dynamine and Pong can also be played against the computer.

Do these two-player games work on a phone or tablet? The pass-and-play games do — tap your move and hand the device over. Pong supports two players on a tablet by dragging on each half of the board. Hook & Ladder and Dynamine are keyboard games and are best on a laptop or desktop.

Is there a signup, a download, or a cost? No. Open the page and play. Nothing is installed, no account is created, and scores are saved only in that browser.
