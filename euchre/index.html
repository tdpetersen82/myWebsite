How to play Euchre

Euchre is the fast partnership trick-taking game of the American Midwest and Ontario, played with a short 24-card deck. You and the player across the table are a team against the other two. One suit becomes trump, then the hand is over in five quick tricks. The deck and the deal

Euchre uses only the 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King and Ace of each suit — 24 cards. Each player is dealt five, and the next card is turned face up. That turned-up card is the offer: it proposes its own suit as trump.

Naming trump

  • Round one. Starting to the dealer's left, each player may order up the turned-up suit as trump, or pass. If someone orders it up, the dealer picks that card into their hand and discards one.
  • Round two. If everyone passes, players get a second chance to name any suit other than the turned-up suit.
  • All pass twice. The hand is dead and is redealt by the next dealer.
  • The team that names trump are the makers. They must take at least three tricks or they get euchred.

The bowers — the rule that trips up new players

Trump does not rank the way you expect. The right bower is the jack of the trump suit, and it is the highest card in the game. The left bower is the other jack of the same color, and for that entire hand it stops being its own suit and becomes trump — the second-highest card.

So if spades are trump, the jack of clubs is a spade. If someone leads clubs, you do not have to play it — it is not a club any more. The rest of trump then runs A, K, Q, 10, 9. Non-trump suits rank normally: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9.

First team to 10 points wins the game.

Going alone

When you name trump you may declare that you are going alone. Your partner lays their hand down and sits out, so the hand is played three-handed. It is the only way to score 4 points at once, but you have no partner to cover you — and being euchred alone still hands the other team 2. Go alone when you hold both bowers plus the ace, or a hand with almost no losers.

Euchre strategy — how to win

  • Count your trump before you order. Three trump with one bower is a normal order-up. Two small trump and no bower is a pass.
  • Remember who gets the turned-up card. Ordering up hands that card to the dealer. If the dealer is an opponent, you are strengthening them — you need a better hand to justify it.
  • Lead trump when you are the maker. Pulling the opponents' trump protects your off-suit aces from being trumped later.
  • Do not trump your partner's winner. If your partner is already taking the trick, throw off a loser instead of wasting a trump.
  • Count the left bower as trump when you count your suit. The most common beginner mistake is treating it as its printed suit and misjudging a hand by a whole trick.
  • Defend hard at 8 or 9 points. A euchre is worth 2 — often the cheapest way to close out a game.

Frequently asked

How many cards are in a Euchre deck? 24 — the 9, 10, J, Q, K and A of each suit.

What is the highest card in Euchre? The right bower, which is the jack of the trump suit. The left bower — the other jack of the same color — is second.

What does euchred mean? The team that named trump won fewer than three tricks, so the defenders score 2 points.
