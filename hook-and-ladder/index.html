How to play Hook & Ladder

Hook & Ladder is a two-player fire truck game about one very long vehicle. The truck is a tiller: a tractor cab pulling a ladder trailer with its own steering seat at the back. One player drives, the other steers the tail, and neither of you can get around the block alone. When the alarm sounds, a building somewhere on the map catches fire and the lane in front of it lights up. Get there, stop with the ladder base inside the marked lane, and raise the ladder before the clock runs out. Save the building and the next call comes in. Lose three buildings and the shift is over.

Controls

  • Player 1, the driver: W gas, S brake, then reverse · A D steer the cab's front wheels.
  • Player 2, the tillerman: ← → steer the trailer's rear wheels · ↑ (or Enter) raise the ladder once the truck is parked in the marked lane.
  • Solo: W/S or the arrow keys drive, A/D steer, Space raises the ladder. The trailer follows on its own.
  • P pauses. On a phone the pad on the left drives and the pad on the right steers the tail and raises the ladder.

Tips & strategy

  • Tiller against the turn. When the driver turns right, the tillerman steers left. The tail swings wide instead of cutting across the corner into the building. It feels backwards for about two calls, then it clicks.
  • Swing wide going in. A 70-foot truck does not turn from the inside lane. Drift toward the far side of the street before the corner and let the cab lead.
  • The ladder base is what counts. The turntable in the middle of the trailer has to be inside the marked lane, not the cab. Pull past the building and let the tail settle in.
  • Stop fully. The ladder will not go up while the truck is rolling. The prompt turns green when you are parked.
  • Clean runs pay. Every call has a bonus for reaching it without scraping a parked car or a wall, on top of five points for every second left on the clock.
  • Reverse is a tool. The tail will not swing back on its own while you sit still. Back up straight, then take the corner again.

About tiller trucks

A tiller, or tractor-drawn aerial, is a ladder truck built like a semi with a second driver at the very back. The tillerman sits above the rear axle and steers the trailer's wheels, which is what lets a rig that long snake through old city streets and around double-parked cars. Fire departments in San Francisco, Los Angeles and Washington still run them for exactly that reason. Driver and tillerman talk over an intercom the whole ride, and in a tight turn the tillerman steers the opposite way to the driver so the tail swings clear. Hook & Ladder puts those two seats on one keyboard.

How do I play Hook & Ladder? Two people share one keyboard. The driver uses W and S for gas and brake and A and D to steer the cab. Can I play alone? Yes. Solo mode lets you drive with the trailer following naturally, and Space raises the ladder. Two-player mode is the full game.
