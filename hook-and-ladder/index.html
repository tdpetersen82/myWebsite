How to play Hook & Ladder

Take a two-person ladder crew into a burning city. One player drives the cab while the other steers the long trailer. Follow the alarm on the minimap, pull into the broad highlighted area beside the building, slow down and deploy. The camera opens into a rooftop rescue. P1 aims a water stream while P2 moves the ladder independently. Cool the fires that block a rescue, bring the people down and finish the remaining flames. Every completed call earns points, with bonuses for quick responses, rescues and clean driving. Lose three buildings and the shift ends.

Controls

  • P1 — driver and hose: W accelerates, S brakes and reverses, A D steer. Space brakes without reversing. On scene, WASD moves the blue hose cursor and Space sprays.
  • P2 — tillerman and rescue: ← → swing the tail. Press Enter alongside the fire to deploy. All four arrow keys then move the amber ladder cursor. Hold Enter over a person to bring them down once the nearby fire is under control.
  • Recovery: R returns a stuck truck to a clear intersection and costs five seconds. P pauses.
  • Solo: WASD drives and aims both tools on scene. Space sprays and rescues. Two players have independent tools.
  • Touch: the left pad drives and aims the hose; the right pad steers and aims the ladder. Use the Spray and Rescue buttons to work.

Tips & strategy

  • Turn, then release. The cab settles onto the street when you release steering near a straight heading. The tail follows with corner assistance; P2 can override it to swing wider or tuck in.
  • Pull alongside. Either the cab or ladder base can enter the highlighted service area. Slow down and press Enter; the crew handles the stop.
  • Use controlled bursts. Release Space between targets to rebuild hose pressure. Partly extinguished fires grow back; finish them before moving on.
  • Clear a rescue path. A red dotted line connects each person to the fire blocking their rescue. P1 cools that fire while P2 positions the ladder. When the person is marked ready, hold Enter to rescue them.
  • Finish the whole call. Clear every fire and rescue every person before the timer expires. Later calls send you farther across the city and add more people.

About tiller trucks

A tiller, or tractor-drawn aerial, is a ladder truck built like a semi with a second driver at the very back. The tillerman sits above the rear axle and steers the trailer's wheels, which is what lets a rig that long snake through old city streets and around double-parked cars. Fire departments in San Francisco, Los Angeles and Washington still run them for exactly that reason. Driver and tillerman talk over an intercom the whole ride, and in a tight turn the tillerman steers the opposite way to the driver so the tail swings clear. Hook & Ladder puts those two seats on one keyboard.
