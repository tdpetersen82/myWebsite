How to play Hook & Ladder

Take a two-person ladder crew into a burning city. One player drives the cab while the other steers the long trailer. Follow the alarm on the minimap, pull into the broad highlighted area beside the building, slow down and deploy. Then work together: keep water flowing, aim at each roof fire and bring the stranded people down. Every completed call earns points, with bonuses for quick responses, rescues and clean driving. Lose three buildings and the shift ends.

Controls

  • P1 — driver and pump: W accelerates, S brakes and reverses, A D steer. Once deployed, hold W to supply water.
  • P2 — tillerman and rescue: ← → steer the rear wheels. Press ↑ when alongside to deploy. Then use ← → to aim the white ring and hold ↑ over each fire or person until their bar empties.
  • Recovery: R returns a stuck truck to a clear intersection and costs five seconds. P pauses.
  • Solo: WASD or arrows drive; A/D or left/right aim once deployed. Hold Space to work. Water is supplied automatically.
  • Touch: the left pad drives and pumps; the right pad steers, aims and works.

Tips & strategy

  • Take the outside of a turn. P2 can steer against the cab's turn to keep the trailer clear of the inside corner. The truck slows automatically as P1 steers.
  • Pull alongside. Either the cab or ladder base can enter the highlighted service area. Slow down and press Up; the crew handles the stop.
  • Keep the pump running. P1 holds W while P2 works the hose. An empty water meter means the flames will not go out.
  • Aim, then hold. The white ring shows where the ladder is working. Move it over a flame or waving person and hold Up. Green checkmarks mean that target is complete.
  • Finish the whole call. Clear every fire and rescue every person before the timer expires. Later calls send you farther across the city and add more people.

About tiller trucks

A tiller, or tractor-drawn aerial, is a ladder truck built like a semi with a second driver at the very back. The tillerman sits above the rear axle and steers the trailer's wheels, which is what lets a rig that long snake through old city streets and around double-parked cars. Fire departments in San Francisco, Los Angeles and Washington still run them for exactly that reason. Driver and tillerman talk over an intercom the whole ride, and in a tight turn the tillerman steers the opposite way to the driver so the tail swings clear. Hook & Ladder puts those two seats on one keyboard.

Frequently asked

How do I play Hook & Ladder? Two people share one keyboard. The driver uses W and S for gas and brake and A and D to steer the cab. The tillerman uses the left and right arrows to steer the rear wheels and the up arrow to raise the ladder. Drive to the burning building, pull alongside and press Up to deploy. What is a tiller fire truck? A ladder truck with a second steering position at the back. The tillerman steers the trailer's rear wheels so a truck the length of a semi can turn down narrow streets. In a turn the tillerman steers opposite to the driver so the tail swings wide.

Can I play alone? Yes. WASD drives the truck and aims the ladder once deployed. Hold Space to work; water is supplied automatically. The game is designed around two-player teamwork.
