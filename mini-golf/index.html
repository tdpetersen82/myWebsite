    How to play

    Drag anywhere on the course, pulling away from your target. The line shows the direction. Release to putt. Drag farther for more power. Tap without dragging to cancel.

    Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space putts. Shift makes finer adjustments. P pauses. M toggles sound.

    Water returns the ball to your last shot and adds one penalty stroke. Amber ramps launch strong shots. Sand slows you down; striped floors push the ball.

    12 strokes is the limit per hole. Practice never changes your round record. Best rounds are saved on this device.
