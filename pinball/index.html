How to play ＋

A D or arrow keys control flippers. Hold Space and release to launch. P pauses; M toggles sound; R starts a new shift.

Drop all three slabs → hit the crusher five times → shoot the conveyor → collect your shipment at the siding. Progress carries between balls.

Each ball gets one seven-second save. Match launch power to the outlined survey lane for a 3,000-point skill shot. Complete survey lanes for up to 5× bonus; chain orbits within eight seconds for bigger haul awards.
