How to play Crash

Crash is the simplest game on the casino floor. A multiplier starts at 1.00x and climbs. At a random moment it busts and the round is over. Place a bet during the countdown, then press Cash out before the bust and you win your bet times the multiplier at that instant. Wait one beat too long and the whole stake is gone. Rounds run back to back, so you can sit out and watch as many as you like.

Controls

  • Pick a bet with the chips, the ½ and 2× buttons, or by typing an amount ($5 to $500)
  • Optionally set an auto cash-out target. The game cashes you out the moment the multiplier reaches it, even if you look away.
  • Space places your bet during the countdown and cashes out while the round is live
  • Esc cancels a bet before the round starts
  • Press the button while a round is running to queue a bet for the next one

The odds

The bust point is 0.99 ÷ (1 − r) where r is a uniform random number, floored to the cent, with a floor of 1.00x and a ceiling of 1,000x. That means the chance the round reaches any multiplier x is exactly 0.99 ÷ x: a 2x round happens 49.5% of the time, a 10x round 9.9%, a 100x round about once in a hundred. About one round in a hundred busts instantly at 1.00x. Cashing out at any target returns 99 cents per dollar over the long run, so the house edge is 1% and it is the same at every target. The multiplier climbs as e0.00006 · ms: about 11.5 seconds to 2x and 38 seconds to 10x.

Provably fair

Every round draws a random 32-byte seed before betting opens. While the round runs, the strip under the graph shows the seed's SHA-256 hash. After the bust it shows the seed itself. The bust point is computed from the first 52 bits of that seed, so you can confirm the hash matches and recompute the multiplier: the outcome was fixed before you bet and could not be changed afterward. Tips & strategy

  • No target beats the edge. Cashing at 1.10x or 10x has the same expected return. What you choose is variance, not value.
  • Low targets, long sessions. A 1.5x target wins two rounds in three and grinds slowly. A 5x target loses four rounds in five and wins big when it hits.
  • Use auto cash-out. The loss in this game is almost always "one more second." A preset target takes that decision away from your thumb.
  • The history strip means nothing. Every round is an independent draw. A run of low busts does not make a big one "due."
  • Set a session budget and walk away when it is spent. Rounds are fifteen seconds; the bankroll goes fast.
