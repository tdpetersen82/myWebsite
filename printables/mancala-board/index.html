Mancala (Kalah)

2 players · 48 seeds (4 per pit)
Anything small works as seeds
Four seeds per small pit to start — use beans, coins, buttons or pebbles (48 total). Your store is on your right.

The rules in brief

  • Pick up all seeds from one of your pits; sow one per pit counter-clockwise
  • Drop into your own store as you pass; skip your opponent's
  • Last seed in your store — free turn
  • Last seed in an empty pit on your side — capture it plus the pit opposite
  • One side empties — the other sweeps their seeds home; most seeds wins

A Mancala board from the printer

Mancala predates boards entirely — it has been played in pits scooped from the ground for well over a thousand years, so a printed sheet is firmly in the tradition. Print it, raid the junk drawer for 48 small counters, and set four in each small pit. Dried beans are the classic; coins, buttons and pebbles all work. Print on card stock and it survives the school term.

How to play, in brief

The full rules are compact enough to sit under the board. On your turn, scoop every seed from one of your six pits and sow them one at a time counter-clockwise, dropping into your own store as you pass and skipping your opponent's. Land the last seed in your store and you go again; land it in an empty pit on your own side and you capture that seed plus everything in the pit directly opposite. When either side's pits empty, the other player sweeps their remaining seeds home, and the fuller store wins.

Which Mancala is this?

The board is standard Kalah — the version nearly everyone in English-speaking countries means by "Mancala", and the version our online game plays. The same printed board works for Oware and other family variants; only the sowing and capture rules change, and our Mancala rules card covers the details and strategy in full.

Frequently asked

What can we use as seeds? Anything small and roughly uniform — 48 of them. Beans, coins, buttons, aquarium gravel.

Which direction do you sow? Counter-clockwise, always, including into your own store and past your opponent's.
