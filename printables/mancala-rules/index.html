How to play Mancala

Mancala is not one game — it is a family played across Africa, the Middle East and Asia for well over a thousand years. The version almost everyone means in English, and the one on this card, is Kalah: two rows of six pits, a store at each end, four seeds per pit at the start.

Sowing

On your turn, choose one of your six pits, lift out every seed in it, and drop them one at a time into each following pit, moving counter-clockwise. You drop a seed into your own store as you pass it, but you always skip your opponent's store.

The two rules that decide games

Free turn. If your final seed lands exactly in your own store, you take another turn immediately. Chaining free turns is the engine of strong play, and a well-set-up board can yield three or four in a row.

Capture. If your final seed lands in an empty pit on your own side, you capture it along with every seed in the pit directly opposite, and all of it goes to your store. A single seed dropped into the right empty pit can take a dozen at once.

Ending the game

The moment either player's six pits are all empty, the round is over. The other player sweeps every remaining seed on their side into their own store. Most seeds wins — 25 or more out of 48 is a win, and 24 each is a draw.

That sweep rule cuts both ways, and it is worth counting before you commit. Emptying your side quickly can force a favourable ending, or hand your opponent a windfall.

Strategy

Look for free turns first, every turn — they are the cheapest advantage available. Keep an empty pit on your side lined up opposite a loaded pit, then land a single seed in it. Before moving, check what you are leaving exposed opposite your opponent's empty pits. Seeds nearer your store need fewer steps to bank and generate more free-turn chances, so loading your right side compounds.

Frequently asked

How many seeds does each pit start with? Four, in all twelve small pits — 48 seeds total.

Do you put seeds in your opponent's store? No. You skip it and drop into your own only.

Can you capture from your opponent's side? Only indirectly — your last seed must land in an empty pit on your side, and you then take the seeds opposite it.
