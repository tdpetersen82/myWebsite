About these printables

Every sheet here is built to do one job well: fit on a single page, stay legible in pencil, and carry its own scoring key so the table is not reaching for a phone mid-game. They print clean in black and white on US Letter or A4.

To make a PDF, press Print on any sheet and choose Save as PDF as the destination — that works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox without installing anything.

Most of these have a playable counterpart on this site, so you can learn the scoring against the computer and then take the sheet to a real table: Yahtzee, Farkle, Spades, Hearts, Euchre, Crazy Eights and Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe.
