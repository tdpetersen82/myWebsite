Scoring a round

  • Hit — flip another card · Stay — bank what’s showing
  • Flip a number you already have — bust: score 0 this round
  • Round score = sum of your number cards
  • ×2 doubles your number cards only — never bonuses or + cards
  • Then add any +2 / +4 / +6 / +8 / +10 modifier cards
  • Seven unique number cards = Flip 7: +15 bonus and the round ends for everyone

How to play Flip 7

Each turn is one decision: hit — flip the next card onto your line — or stay and bank what's showing. Number cards pile up your round score, but flip a number you already have and you bust: everything gone, out of the round. The deck is built for temptation — high numbers are plentiful (twelve 12s, but only a single 1), so the next flip is usually big and often fatal.

The special cards

Three action cards shove people around: Freeze forces its target to bank and sit out; Flip Three forces three consecutive flips, ready or not; Second Chance quietly saves you from one duplicate — discard the pair and keep going. Modifiers sweeten a stay: +2 through +10 add flat points, and ×2 doubles your number cards (never the modifiers, never the bonus — the pad's box settles that argument in advance).

The Flip 7 itself

Seven unique number cards in one line ends the round on the spot for the whole table and pays a +15 bonus. Chasing it is how brave rounds become zeros; landing it is why the game is named after it.

Frequently asked

Do action cards count toward the seven? No — only number cards. A line of six numbers plus a Freeze is still six.

Can two players score 200+ in the same round? Yes, and the higher total takes the game — finishing the round matters, so keep writing totals to the end.
