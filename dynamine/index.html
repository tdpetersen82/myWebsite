Break through the mine

Plant dynamite, clear collapsed rock, and trap the creatures roaming the galleries. Defeat every creature to reveal the green exit, then step into it. The first five mines have designed layouts; deeper mines vary on each run. You have three lives, with no Adventure time limit.

Controls

Arrows / WASD: move. Space: plant dynamite. F: use your equipped tool. P: pause. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Know your blast

  • Two-second fuse. Find your escape before planting. Fire travels in straight lines; rock and bedrock stop it.
  • Linked shafts. Plant directly on a shaft to send a one-tile blast out of every shaft. Keep clear of all of them.
  • Mine tools. A flare launcher fires straight ahead. A blasting plunger detonates your first planted bomb early. Tools last 40 seconds.
  • Supplies. Extra dynamite, blast reach, boots, and a protective helmet are hidden under rock. The helmet absorbs one hit.

Battle

Play the computer or a friend on one keyboard. Blue uses WASD / Space / F; Red uses arrows / Enter / Right Shift. Last miner standing takes the round; first to three wins. The roof starts closing in with 45 seconds left.

Frequently asked

How do I play Dynamine? Move with arrows or WASD. Space plants dynamite with a two-second fuse. Retreat around a corner before it explodes. Clear every creature to reveal the green exit, then enter it.

Can I play Dynamine with two players? Yes. Local Battle uses WASD and Space for Blue, arrows and Enter for Red. F and Right Shift use their equipped tools. You can also battle the computer.

Does my high score save? Yes. Your best Adventure score saves in this browser.
